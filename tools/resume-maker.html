    Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS): What Actually Matters

    Many employers use an applicant tracking system to collect and search resumes. Parsers differ in quality, so the safest approach is to make your resume easy to read in plain text. In general:

    • Use the "Strict ATS Text" Format. In the Style tab, change the Document Format Strategy to ATS. This removes all columns, background colors, and complex structures, forcing a clean, top-to-bottom text flow that every machine can read perfectly.
    • Use standard section names such as Work Experience, Education and Skills. This tool does so automatically.
    • Keep text as text. Information inside images, including a photo, cannot be read. Skill “bars” and rating dots convey nothing to a parser, so this tool does not offer them.
    • Match the job description honestly. Use the same terms the advertisement uses for skills you genuinely have.

    Privacy: Where Your Data Lives

    All processing happens directly inside your web browser. Your text and photo are kept in this browser’s local storage so you can return later, and they are never sent to a server by this page. Clearing your browser data, using a private window, or switching devices will remove them, so use Download backup (.json) to keep a safe copy on your hard drive. If you use a shared public computer, always click Clear everything before you close the tab.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

    Can I build a Marriage Biodata or Academic CV here?
    Yes! This is an all-in-one document builder. Select "Marriage & Personal Biodata" or "Educational & Academic CV" from the dropdown in the Content tab, and the builder will instantly load specialized fields like Family Background, Partner Preferences, Research Experience, and Publications.
    Is this resume maker really free, and do I need to sign up?
    Yes, it is 100% free to use and there is no sign-up, no watermark, and no download limit. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so there is no account to create. You can build, restyle, and save as many resumes as you like.
    How do I download my document as a photo (PNG)?
    Go to the Download tab and click Download as Photo (.png). The builder will process your document entirely inside your browser and save a high-quality image file to your device, perfect for sharing on WhatsApp or social media.
    How do I download my resume as a PDF?
    Open the Download tab and select Print / Save as PDF. In the print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" as the destination, set margins to "Default" or "None", and untick "Headers and footers". If your browser offers a background graphics option, enable it so your banner and sidebar colours appear beautifully.
    How do I force my resume to fit on exactly one page?
    In the Style tab, look for the "Fix Page Count" dropdown next to the font size slider. Change it from "Auto" to "Exactly 1 Page". This will lock the document height. You may need to lower your font size, select a "Compact" spacing density, or trim some bullet points to make sure your text fits inside the 1-page boundary.
    Can I edit my resume later or move it to another device?
    Yes. Your work is saved automatically in the same browser. To continue on another device or keep a safe offline copy, use Download backup (.json) on the Download tab. Then, open the tool on your other device and click Load backup. This small file perfectly restores all your content and style choices.
