Exam Marks & Percentage Calculator

How to Calculate Your Exam Percentage

Most students need to calculate their percentage at some point, whether it's for exam results, college admissions, or scholarship applications. Many admission forms and job applications ask applicants to provide their percentage score.

The marks percentage calculator above does the math for you instantly. If you're curious about how the calculation works, here is the simple formula behind it, along with a few practical examples.

How to Use the Calculator

  • Quick Total %: enter the marks you obtained and the maximum marks. Use this when you already know your grand total.
  • Subject Marksheet: enter marks per subject (maximum defaults to 100 and can be changed). Add more subjects with the button, leave a subject blank to skip it, or tick "Best of 5" to count only your five highest subjects when you have more than five.
  • CBSE CGPA to %: enter your CGPA out of 10 and choose the multiplier. The CBSE standard multiplier is 9.5.

The result updates as you type and shows your percentage, the division band, how far you are from the next band and, where relevant, how many marks you need to reach the pass mark.

Percentage Formula with Examples

You can calculate your percentage yourself using a simple math formula. You only need two numbers: the marks you earned, and the total marks possible. Here is the standard marks to percentage formula:

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example 1: Percentage of 420 out of 500

If your exams consisted of 5 subjects worth 100 marks each, your total maximum marks would be 500. Let's say you scored exactly 420 marks. To find the percentage:

  • Step 1: Divide 420 by 500 (420 ÷ 500 = 0.84)
  • Step 2: Multiply 0.84 by 100
  • Result: 84% (1st Division with Distinction)

Example 2: Percentage of 350 out of 600

If your board requires you to take 6 subjects worth 100 marks each, your total possible score is 600. If you obtained 350 marks in total, the math looks like this:

  • Step 1: Divide 350 by 600 (350 ÷ 600 = 0.5833)
  • Step 2: Multiply 0.5833 by 100
  • Result: 58.33% (2nd Division)

How to Calculate Percentage of 5 Subjects

If you just got your mark sheet and want to find your total percentage across 5 subjects, follow these steps:

  1. Check the maximum marks for each subject (usually 100).
  2. Add the maximum marks together (100 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 = 500 total).
  3. Add up the marks you actually scored in all 5 subjects (e.g., 75 + 82 + 68 + 90 + 71 = 386).
  4. Divide your score by the total marks (386 ÷ 500 = 0.772).
  5. Multiply by 100 to get your final percentage (77.2%).

Add the marks first and divide once. Averaging the five individual percentages gives the same answer only when every subject has the same maximum; with different maximums, such as one 50-mark and four 100-mark papers, always divide total marks by total maximum.

State and Central Board Calculations

How to Calculate Percentage in CBSE

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) often issues a CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) instead of a straight percentage. If an admission form asks for a percentage, you have to convert it.

The official CBSE standard rule is to multiply your CGPA by 9.5. For example, if your report card shows a CGPA of 8.4, you multiply 8.4 by 9.5 to get 79.80%.

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

How to Calculate Percentage in Bihar Board (BSEB)

The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) typically calculates class 10th (Matric) and 12th (Inter) results out of 500 total marks. They use a straightforward division method rather than a grading system.

If you score 300 out of 500 on your Bihar Board exams, your calculation is (300 ÷ 500) × 100, giving you exactly 60%. Scoring 60% or above in BSEB places you in the "1st Division" category.

Assumptions and Limitations

  • Percentage is total marks obtained divided by total maximum marks, not the average of subject percentages.
  • The 33% pass mark and the division bands are common norms. Boards and universities set their own pass marks, grace marks and subject-wise passing rules.
  • The CGPA conversion uses the multiplier you select; the 9.5 option is the CBSE standard. Other boards and universities may use different formulas.
  • Results are shown to two decimal places. Some institutions round or truncate differently, so use the exact figure printed on your official marksheet for any application.
  • The calculation runs entirely in your browser; the values you enter are not sent anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between percentage and CGPA?

A percentage is a score out of 100 that shows your exact academic performance. A CGPA is a grading system that averages your performance on a scale of 1 to 10.

How do I calculate my percentage from marks?

Divide the total marks you earned by the maximum possible marks, and then multiply that number by 100.

How do I convert CBSE CGPA to percentage?

Multiply your CGPA by 9.5. For example, a CGPA of 8.4 gives 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.80%. This is the standard CBSE conversion.

What is the pass percentage?

Many boards use 33% as the minimum pass mark, but the rule varies by board, class and subject, and some require passing each subject separately. Check your board's official rules.

Can I round up my percentage for a job application?

No, it is highly recommended not to do this. For example, if a government job requires a minimum of 60%, and you score 59.9%, you do not meet the criteria. Always enter the exact decimal you earned to avoid rejection.

Conclusion

Calculating a percentage takes one division and one multiplication, but the right inputs matter: total marks over total maximum for a mark sheet, and CGPA times 9.5 for a CBSE grade. Use the calculator to check your result, to see how far you are from the next division and to see how many marks you would need to reach a target.
