How to Use the Multicolour Screen Flashlight

Unlike the camera LED on the back of your phone, which can only shine white, your display can show millions of colors. The Color Screen Flashlight turns your whole screen into a customizable light on phones, tablets and computers.

  • Solid Light: A steady glow in any of 18 preset colors or a custom color. Use warm white or night red for reading, or cool white when the power goes out.
  • Color Cycle: Fades smoothly through a palette such as Rainbow, Sunset, Ocean or Aurora. Great for mood lighting and photo backdrops.
  • Multicolour Flash: Jumps between the colors of a palette, like Police, Neon or Party. Good for celebrations and attention-grabbing signals.
  • Strobe: Flashes your chosen color against black at an adjustable, safety-limited speed.

Why Use a Screen Flashlight Instead of the LED?

The hardware LED is bright and focused, which casts harsh shadows. A screen flashlight gives a diffused, soft-box effect: the light spreads across a large surface instead of a tiny bulb, so shadows are much softer.

That makes it handy for macro photography, lighting a tent without blinding anyone, and preserving night vision with a deep red screen.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can this app change the color of my phone's camera LED flash?
No. The LED flashlight on the back of a smartphone is made of white light-emitting diodes, so its hardware cannot change colors. This tool uses your device's screen to emit colored light instead.
Why is the screen flashlight not bright enough?
Web browsers cannot control your device's hardware brightness for security reasons. For the strongest light, turn the screen brightness to 100% using your phone's quick settings or your laptop's brightness keys.
Will the screen go to sleep while the flashlight is on?
On browsers that support the Screen Wake Lock API, the screen stays on while the flashlight is running. On browsers without it, your normal screen timeout still applies.
Does it work on iPhone, Android, tablets and laptops?
Yes. It works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. Some browsers, such as Safari on iPhone, cannot enter true fullscreen, but the light still fills the whole visible screen.
How do I exit the flashlight?
Tap or click anywhere on the screen, or press Esc, Enter or Space on a keyboard.
