How to Use the Multicolour Screen Flashlight

Unlike the physical camera LED on the back of your smartphone, which is restricted to a single white color temperature, your device's high-resolution display is capable of outputting millions of colors. The Color Screen Flashlight utilizes the HTML5 Fullscreen API to safely transform your entire display into a powerful, customizable light source.

  • Solid Light Mode: Emits a steady, continuous beam of light. Perfect for reading in the dark without straining your eyes (use warm colors like red or orange) or illuminating a room when the power goes out.
  • Strobe & Flashing Mode: Rapidly alternates the screen between your selected color and total blackness. This is highly useful for emergencies, grabbing attention on the side of a road, or creating dynamic lighting effects.
  • Custom Hex Selection: Use the built-in color picker to find the exact hue and saturation you need for photography lighting or mood setting.

Why Use a Screen Flashlight Instead of the LED?

While the hardware camera LED is extremely bright and focused, it produces a harsh, blinding spotlight effect that causes heavy shadows. A screen flashlight provides a diffused, soft-box effect. Because the light is emitted evenly across a large 6-inch surface area rather than a tiny 3mm bulb, it creates much softer shadows.

This makes screen flashlights ideal for macro photography, lighting up a tent without blinding your camping partners, or preserving night vision (by utilizing a pure red screen color).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can this app change the color of my phone's camera LED flash?
No. The physical LED flashlight on the back of smartphones consists of standard white light-emitting diodes, meaning their hardware cannot physically change colors. This tool circumvents that hardware limitation by using your device's screen to emit colored light instead.
Why is the screen flashlight not bright enough?
Web browsers cannot automatically control your device's hardware brightness due to modern sandboxing and security restrictions. To get the maximum flashlight effect, you must manually swipe down your phone's notification panel (or use your laptop's brightness keys) and turn the screen brightness to 100%.
Will leaving the screen flashlight on cause my phone to go to sleep?
No. This tool utilizes the modern Screen Wake Lock API to prevent your device from turning off the screen while the flashlight mode is actively running. As soon as you exit the flashlight mode, normal screen timeout settings resume.
